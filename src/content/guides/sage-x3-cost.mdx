## Deployment

Sage X3 can run as a fully managed SaaS offering, in a private cloud or on-premise. If you host it yourself, hosting, backup and upgrade effort become your costs, so include them.

## Implementation scope

Mysoft publishes two delivery approaches for Sage X3: <MysoftLink to="mysoft-rapid-deploy" position="x3-cost-body">Kickstart, a standard delivery pre-configured with best-practice settings</MysoftLink>, and Toolkit, which builds on it for extended scope. Its published options are 80 service days over six calendar months (Kickstart, which excludes manufacturing), 110 service days over nine months, and 140 service days over twelve months. Mysoft also publishes a <MysoftLink to="mysoft-x3-cost" position="x3-cost-body">calculator for extended scope</MysoftLink>.

## Ongoing costs

After go-live, budget for subscription or maintenance, partner support, hosting if you run it yourself, and upgrades. Add-ons such as document automation have their own subscriptions.

## Build the five-year view

Compare options on the same scope over five years. The [ERP total cost of ownership guide](/guides/erp-tco/) sets out the categories to include.
