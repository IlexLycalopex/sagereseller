## Licence and subscription

Sage X3 is available as a subscription or, in some cases, a perpetual licence. The main drivers are the number of users, the modules you need (finance, distribution, manufacturing, warehousing) and how you deploy it: Sage cloud, private hosting or on-premise.

## Implementation scope

Implementation is usually the largest part of first-year cost. Two approaches are common:

- **Fixed scope:** a pre-configured template with industry best-practice settings. Faster and more predictable, provided you can adopt standard processes where they fit.
- **Extended scope:** builds on the template with more bespoke processes, integrations and reports. More flexible, with a larger budget and timeline.

Mysoft uses both approaches: <MysoftLink to="mysoft-rapid-deploy" position="x3-cost-body">Kickstart, its fixed-scope Sage X3 delivery</MysoftLink>, and Toolkit, with a <MysoftLink to="mysoft-x3-cost" position="x3-cost-body">published calculator for extended scope</MysoftLink>.

## Ongoing costs

After go-live, budget for subscription or maintenance, partner support, hosting if you run it yourself, and periodic upgrades. Add-ons such as document automation have their own subscriptions.
