## When Sage Intacct fits

Sage Intacct suits Sage 200 users whose main pain is financial: consolidating several companies, reporting by department or project, managing subscriptions or funds, or closing faster. It is cloud only, so it also suits businesses that want to stop running servers.

## When Sage X3 fits

Sage X3 suits Sage 200 users whose operations have grown: more stock lines, several warehouses, lot or serial traceability, manufacturing, or trading in several countries. It keeps finance and operations in one system and can run in the cloud or on-premise.

## Data migration choices

Migration scope is one of the biggest drivers of cost and timeline. Agree it before you ask for quotes, so partners price the same thing:

- **Balances only:** fastest and cheapest, and often enough.
- **Balances plus summarised history:** gives year-on-year comparisons in the new system.
- **Detailed history:** most expensive; only worth it where there is a clear reporting or compliance need.

## Timeline

A finance-led move to Sage Intacct is usually shorter than a move to Sage X3, because there is less operational scope. For Sage X3, fixed-scope approaches with pre-configured templates are the quickest route. Either way, the date you choose for go-live, and the time your team can give the project, matter as much as the product.
