## The licence model

Sage Intacct is sold as an annual subscription. The main drivers are:

- **Core financials:** general ledger, payables, receivables, cash management and reporting.
- **Additional modules:** for example multi-entity consolidation, project accounting, revenue management, or inventory.
- **Entities and users:** more entities and more full users increase the subscription.
- **Contract term:** longer terms can affect pricing and renewal terms.

## Implementation drivers

Implementation is quoted separately by your partner. What moves the number most:

- The number of entities and how intercompany trading works.
- Integrations to CRM, payroll, billing, banking or ecommerce.
- The volume and quality of data you migrate.
- Reports and dashboards you need on day one.
- How much of the work your own team takes on.

## Add-ons

Many businesses connect marketplace add-ons for AP automation, planning and budgeting, payroll, or spend management. Each usually has its own subscription. Include them in the comparison so the total is complete.

## Year one versus ongoing

Year one carries the implementation and the first subscription. From year two, the cost is mainly subscription, support and any add-ons.
