## How a quote is built

Sage Intacct quotes are based on:

- **Modules:** such as core financials, accounts payable and receivable, cash management, general ledger, purchasing, order management, inventory, project accounting, multi-entity consolidation and revenue recognition.
- **Users and entities:** more of either increases the subscription.
- **Contract term:** the length of the commitment.

## Implementation

Implementation is quoted separately by your partner. Ask each partner to price the same scope: entities, integrations, reports and the amount of history you will migrate.

## Add-ons

Sage says more than 75% of Sage Intacct customers have integrated two or more solutions, and its marketplace lists more than 350 partners. Add-ons can carry their own subscriptions, so include them in the comparison.

## Year one versus ongoing

Year one carries implementation and the first subscription. After that, the cost is mainly subscription, support and add-ons. A five-year view is the fairest comparison; the [ERP total cost of ownership guide](/guides/erp-tco/) shows how.
