## The signals checklist

Tick the ones that apply to you today:

- Month-end close regularly takes more than five working days.
- You consolidate two or more entities in spreadsheets.
- Board or investor reporting depends on one person's workbook.
- You have added, or are about to add, another currency or country.
- You bill subscriptions, projects or grants and track them outside the ledger.
- You use several add-on apps to fill gaps, and they do not agree with each other.
- Your auditors have raised controls or audit trail points.

One or two may be manageable. Three or more usually means the cost of staying is higher than the cost of moving.

## Options by business model

- **Services, SaaS, non-profit and financial services:** a finance-first system such as Sage Intacct, with strong multi-entity and dimensional reporting.
- **Distribution and wholesale:** an ERP with purchasing, stock and order processing, such as Sage X3, or Sage Intacct with an inventory add-on if stock is simple.
- **Manufacturing:** an ERP with production and MRP, such as Sage X3.

Not sure which applies? The [selector](/which-sage/) takes about three minutes.

## How much effort migration takes

Moving off Xero or QuickBooks is usually simpler than replacing an older ERP, because the data is cleaner and the processes are lighter. The main work is designing the chart of accounts and dimensions, setting up entities, and deciding how much history to bring.

## The cost of waiting

Every month on a system you have outgrown costs time in the finance team and slows the decisions that depend on your numbers. Our [cost of doing nothing guide](/guides/cost-of-doing-nothing/) includes a simple calculator.
