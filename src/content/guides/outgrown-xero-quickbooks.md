## The signals checklist

Tick the ones that apply to you today:

- Month-end close regularly takes more than five working days.
- You consolidate two or more entities in spreadsheets.
- Board or investor reporting depends on one person's workbook.
- You have added, or are about to add, another currency or country.
- You bill subscriptions, projects or grants and track them outside the ledger.
- You use several add-on apps to fill gaps, and they do not agree with each other.
- Your auditors have raised controls or audit trail points.

The more that apply, the more it is worth measuring what staying put costs. The [cost of doing nothing guide](/guides/cost-of-doing-nothing/) helps you put a number on it.

## Options by business model

- **Services, SaaS, non-profit and financial services:** a finance system such as Sage Intacct, which automates multi-entity consolidation and includes subscription billing, revenue recognition and project accounting.
- **Distribution and wholesale:** Sage X3, which includes advanced warehouse management, or Sage Intacct, which includes inventory management across multiple locations, if stock is simple.
- **Manufacturing:** Sage X3, which supports process and discrete manufacturing.

Not sure which applies? The [selector](/which-sage/) takes about three minutes.

## What the migration involves

The main decisions are how to design the chart of accounts and reporting dimensions, how to set up your entities, and how much history to bring across. Agree them before you ask for quotes, so every partner prices the same scope.
