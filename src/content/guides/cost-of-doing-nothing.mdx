## Where the cost comes from

- **Manual effort:** rekeying, spreadsheet consolidation, chasing approvals, manual reconciliations.
- **Close speed:** every extra day at month-end delays the numbers the business runs on.
- **Errors:** manual processes create errors that take time to find and fix, and some reach customers, suppliers or auditors.
- **Compliance risk:** late or inaccurate VAT returns, for example, can lead to penalty points and penalties under HMRC's regime.
- **Key-person risk:** processes that live in one person's spreadsheets stop when that person is away or leaves.

## Using the number

If the annual figure is larger than you expected, compare it with the five-year cost of a new system using the [ERP total cost of ownership guide](/guides/erp-tco/).
