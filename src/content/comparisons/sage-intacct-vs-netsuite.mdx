## Where NetSuite is stronger

NetSuite's advantage is breadth from one vendor. Its module list covers finance, procurement, manufacturing, inventory, order management, warehouse management, supply chain, CRM, project resource management, HR, ecommerce and marketing automation. A business that wants all of those from a single supplier can license them as optional modules on one platform.

For groups with many subsidiaries, NetSuite OneWorld is designed for standardising global operations.

NetSuite is a sensible choice when:

- You want CRM, inventory, warehousing and ecommerce from the same vendor as finance.
- Manufacturing and order management need to sit in the same system as the ledger.
- You are standardising many subsidiaries on one global platform.

## Where Sage Intacct is stronger

Sage Intacct is financial management software first. Sage describes it as automating multi-entity consolidation across currencies, whether you have two entities or hundreds. Subscription billing supports more than 500 subscription, usage and hybrid billing models, with revenue recognition to ASC 606 and IFRS 15, and project accounting is included.

Sage has also added AI agents, including an AP automation agent that codes bills and matches purchase orders, and a financial intelligence agent that answers questions about your financial data in plain language.

Rather than supplying every operational module itself, Sage Intacct connects to specialist tools: the Sage Intacct Marketplace lists more than 350 partners, and Sage says more than 75% of Sage Intacct customers have integrated two or more solutions. You can read <MysoftLink to="mysoft-intacct-implementation" position="body">how Mysoft implements Sage Intacct</MysoftLink> for an example of partner-led delivery.

Sage Intacct is a sensible choice when:

- Your complexity is in entities, currencies, revenue or projects rather than stock.
- You want finance to own the core system, with operational tools connected around it.

## Cost and implementation

Sage Intacct is quoted on modules, users, entities and contract term. NetSuite's annual licence combines the core platform, optional modules and users, with a one-time implementation fee. Ask both suppliers to price the same scope, then build a five-year view including implementation, renewals, support and add-ons. The [Sage Intacct cost guide](/guides/sage-intacct-cost-uk/) and the [ERP total cost of ownership guide](/guides/erp-tco/) show how.
