## Where Acumatica is stronger

Acumatica's licence model is its clearest difference. It charges by the applications you use and a resource tier based on activity, not by user, so every employee can have access. For manufacturers with many occasional users on the shop floor, in the warehouse or in the field, that can change the economics.

It also offers industry editions, including construction, retail and field services, and a modern browser-based interface. In North America it has a large partner network.

Acumatica is a sensible choice when:

- Many people need occasional access and per-user pricing would be costly.
- An Acumatica industry edition matches your business closely.
- Your operations are mainly in the US.

## Where Sage X3 is stronger

Sage X3 has depth in process manufacturing: recipes, formulas, batch control, quality and production costing are in the core product. For food and drink, chemicals and life sciences, that depth often decides the shortlist.

It handles multi-country operations through localisations for many legislations, and in the UK it has an established partner base with long Sage X3 experience. Mysoft, for example, was the UK's first Sage X3 partner.

Its ecosystem includes specialist add-ons built for Sage X3, such as <MysoftLink to="mysoft-x3clouddocs" position="body">X3CloudDocs, Mysoft's document and invoice automation</MysoftLink>.

## Cost and implementation

The two licence models are hard to compare directly, so model your real user mix and transaction volume over five years. For Sage X3, Mysoft publishes a <MysoftLink to="mysoft-x3-cost" position="body">Sage X3 cost calculator</MysoftLink>.
