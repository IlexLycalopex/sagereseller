## Where Acumatica is stronger

Acumatica's licence model is its clearest difference. It charges by the applications you license and a resource tier based on your activity, with unlimited users on every tier. For businesses with many occasional users on the shop floor, in the warehouse or in the field, that can change the economics compared with per-user licensing.

It offers industry editions for construction, distribution, general business, manufacturing, professional services and retail, and can be deployed as SaaS, in a private cloud or on-premise.

## Where Sage X3 is stronger

Sage X3 supports process as well as discrete manufacturing. Sage lists advanced warehouse management, manufacturing planning and end-to-end batch genealogy, with lot and serial numbers tracked to their final destination and expiry dates calculated as required. It provides multi-site, multi-currency and multi-country management, with support for multiple legislations.

Its ecosystem includes add-ons built for Sage X3, such as <MysoftLink to="mysoft-x3clouddocs" position="body">X3CloudDocs, Mysoft's document and invoice automation</MysoftLink>.

## Cost and implementation

The two licence models are hard to compare directly, so model your real user mix and transaction volume over five years. Mysoft publishes a <MysoftLink to="mysoft-x3-cost" position="body">Sage X3 cost calculator</MysoftLink>. The [ERP total cost of ownership guide](/guides/erp-tco/) sets out the categories to include.
