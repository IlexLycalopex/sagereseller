## Where Rillet is stronger

Rillet is built as an AI-native ERP for venture-backed SaaS companies. Its pitch is automation in the ledger itself: revenue schedules generated from CRM contracts, automated journal entries and accruals, and a close measured in hours rather than days.

For a small SaaS finance team whose revenue model is subscription and usage, and whose contracts live in Salesforce or HubSpot, that focus can remove a lot of manual work. The product and the workflow are designed around modern SaaS finance.

## Where Sage Intacct is stronger

Sage Intacct is a mature platform with an audit trail and controls that auditors across the UK and US already know. For finance leaders who answer to boards, lenders and auditors, that track record reduces risk.

It is also broader. Sage Intacct serves services firms, non-profits, financial services and SaaS, so a business that adds a services arm, acquires a company with a different model, or opens a UK entity will not outgrow it. It has UK and US partner networks, a large marketplace, and a published AI roadmap from a global vendor. Both are quoted on a subscription basis. Consider the cost of change as well as the licence: an early-stage choice that has to be replaced after an acquisition or international expansion costs a second implementation. Our [ERP total cost of ownership guide](/guides/erp-tco/) covers that.
