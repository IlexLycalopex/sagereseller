## Where Campfire is stronger

Campfire is a new, AI-first ERP designed around the general ledger. It offers multi-entity consolidation, revenue automation and close management, with conversational AI reporting as a central part of the experience. The interface is modern and designed from scratch.

For US technology scale-ups moving off entry-level accounting, that combination is attractive: a system that feels current, with automation built in rather than added on.

## Where Sage Intacct is stronger

Sage Intacct has years of use in multi-entity finance teams, and auditors across the UK and US know it well. Its consolidation, dimensional reporting and controls are established, and it serves many business models, not only technology companies.

For groups with UK entities, Sage Intacct's UK edition and UK partner network are a practical advantage. Its marketplace and API give finance teams proven integrations for payroll, planning, spend and CRM.

## Cost and implementation

Both are quote-based subscriptions. Compare a five-year cost for the same scope, and factor in the risk of switching again if the business outgrows the first choice. See the [ERP total cost of ownership guide](/guides/erp-tco/).
