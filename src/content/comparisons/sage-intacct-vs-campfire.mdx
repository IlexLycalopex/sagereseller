## Where Campfire is stronger

Campfire is an AI-native general ledger and ERP, founded in 2023 out of Y Combinator, that has raised around $103 million. It combines multi-entity and multi-currency consolidation, ASC 606 revenue automation across subscription, usage, milestone and transaction billing, close management, and an AI layer that includes its Ember assistant.

For a technology company moving off entry-level accounting, that combination of a modern ledger with conversational AI reporting built in is the main attraction. Campfire positions itself against established cloud ERPs such as NetSuite.

## Where Sage Intacct is stronger

Sage Intacct includes project accounting as well as subscription billing across more than 500 subscription, usage and hybrid billing models, with revenue recognition to ASC 606 and IFRS 15. It automates multi-entity consolidation across currencies.

For UK entities, Sage Intacct submits VAT returns to HMRC through Sage Regulatory Reporting. The Sage Intacct Marketplace lists more than 350 integration partners, and Sage has added AI agents for AP automation and financial questions.
