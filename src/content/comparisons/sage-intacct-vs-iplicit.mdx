## Where iplicit is stronger

iplicit describes itself as cloud accounting software for growing businesses and non-profit organisations. For charities, it highlights compliance with the Statement of Recommended Practice (SORP) and FRS 102, with handling of funds and multiple reporting criteria, and integrations with donor, membership and grant management systems.

It supports real-time consolidation across more than 300 entities, with intercompany eliminations and multi-currency, and is HMRC-recognised Making Tax Digital software.

iplicit is a sensible choice when:

- SORP fund reporting is your headline requirement.
- You need your finance system to integrate with donor, membership or grant systems.

## Where Sage Intacct is stronger

Sage Intacct combines multi-entity consolidation with subscription billing (more than 500 subscription, usage and hybrid billing models), revenue recognition to ASC 606 and IFRS 15, and project accounting. For a services group or a non-profit with trading subsidiaries, that breadth can matter.

Its ecosystem is large: the Sage Intacct Marketplace lists more than 350 partners. Sage Intacct was developed in the US and acquired by Sage in 2017, then localised for the UK, so it serves groups with entities on both sides of the Atlantic.
