## Where iplicit is stronger

iplicit is built in the UK for UK organisations. Its development and support are UK-focused, and it has built a strong position with UK charities and multi-entity mid-market groups. For a UK-only organisation, that focus can mean a product and a vendor that feel closer to home.

It handles multi-entity consolidation natively and emphasises SORP reporting for charities. A smaller vendor can also be more responsive to feedback from individual customers.

iplicit is a sensible choice when:

- You operate only in the UK and have no plans to expand overseas.
- You prefer a UK-built product from a UK-focused vendor.
- You are a charity that wants SORP reporting as a headline feature.

## Where Sage Intacct is stronger

Sage Intacct operates at a different scale. It has a large customer base in both the US and the UK, a broad marketplace of proven integrations, and the backing of a global vendor with a published AI roadmap. For groups that expect to grow into new countries, particularly the US, that reach matters.

Its dimensional general ledger gives finance teams flexible reporting across entities, departments, projects, funds and grants without restructuring the chart of accounts. For non-profits, restricted funds and grants are tracked as dimensions and reported alongside everything else. Mysoft covers <MysoftLink to="mysoft-industry-nonprofit" position="body">Sage Intacct for non-profits</MysoftLink> in more depth.

The size of the ecosystem is a practical advantage: payroll, planning, AP automation, spend management and CRM integrations are available off the shelf rather than built.
