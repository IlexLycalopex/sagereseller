## Where Dynamics 365 Finance and Operations is stronger

Microsoft's enterprise ERP (now sold as Dynamics 365 Finance and Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management) is built for scale. Large, global groups with many countries, complex supply chains and demanding compliance needs will find very broad functional coverage.

It also fits naturally into a Microsoft-first IT estate. If your data platform is on Azure, your teams use Microsoft 365 and your developers build on the Power Platform, Dynamics 365 gives you one technology stack.

It is a sensible choice when:

- You operate at enterprise scale across many countries.
- You have, or plan to build, an internal team to own the ERP.
- Microsoft alignment is a strategic requirement for IT.

## Where Sage X3 is stronger

Sage X3 is sized for the mid-market. It covers finance, purchasing, sales, stock, warehousing and manufacturing (both process and discrete) in one system, without the programme overhead that often comes with enterprise ERP.

Deployment choice is a real difference. Sage X3 can run in the cloud, in private hosting or on-premise, which matters to businesses with plant-floor integrations, data residency requirements or an existing hosting strategy.

In Mysoft's experience, mid-market manufacturers and distributors reach go-live faster and with a smaller project team on Sage X3, and the running cost is lower. <MysoftLink to="mysoft-rapid-deploy" position="body">Mysoft's fixed-scope Kickstart approach to Sage X3</MysoftLink> is designed for exactly that.

## Cost and implementation

Microsoft publishes per-user prices for Dynamics 365, with minimum licence counts. Sage X3 is quoted by users and modules. In both cases, licences are only part of the cost: implementation, hosting, support and upgrades usually decide the five-year total.
