## Where Dynamics 365 Finance and Operations is stronger

Microsoft's enterprise ERP is now sold as Dynamics 365 Finance and Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management. Microsoft lists Supply Chain Management capabilities spanning product information management, forecasting, planning, inventory, sales and procurement, as well as complex manufacturing, asset maintenance, warehousing, transportation management and costing. Finance adds planning, budgeting and forecasting with Copilot.

The Dynamics 365 applications connect with Microsoft 365, Azure and the Power Platform, which suits an IT estate that is standardising on Microsoft.

It is a sensible choice when:

- Microsoft alignment is a strategic requirement for IT.
- You need asset maintenance and transportation management in the same system.

## Where Sage X3 is stronger

Sage X3 gives you a choice of deployment: a fully managed SaaS offering, private cloud or on-premises. That matters for businesses with plant-floor integrations, data residency requirements or an existing hosting strategy.

It supports both process and discrete manufacturing, and Sage lists advanced warehouse management, manufacturing planning and end-to-end batch genealogy. Lot and serial numbers can be tracked to their final destination, with expiry dates calculated as required.

Mysoft publishes its <MysoftLink to="mysoft-methods" position="body">Sage X3 delivery options</MysoftLink>: from 80 service days over six calendar months (Kickstart, which excludes manufacturing) to 140 service days over twelve months.

## Cost and implementation

Microsoft publishes UK prices for Dynamics 365 Finance and licenses its core applications as named-user subscriptions. Sage X3 is quoted. In both cases, implementation, hosting, support and upgrades decide the five-year total. See the [ERP total cost of ownership guide](/guides/erp-tco/).
