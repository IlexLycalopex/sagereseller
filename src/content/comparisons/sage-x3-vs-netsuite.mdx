## Where NetSuite is stronger

NetSuite licenses a broad set of optional modules on one cloud platform, including CRM, ecommerce (SuiteCommerce), order management, inventory, warehouse management and manufacturing. SuiteCommerce is unified with NetSuite's financials, order and inventory management and CRM, which suits product businesses whose complexity sits in sales channels.

NetSuite is cloud-only and vendor-managed, and modules can be licensed at any time during the contract.

## Where Sage X3 is stronger

Sage X3 supports both process and discrete manufacturing. Sage lists advanced warehouse management, manufacturing planning and end-to-end batch genealogy, and lot and serial numbers can be tracked to their final destination, with expiry dates calculated as required. Sage positions this traceability for regulated industries such as food and beverage, pharmaceuticals and chemicals.

Deployment choice is the other difference. Sage X3 can run as SaaS, in a private cloud or on-premise; NetSuite is cloud-only. See <MysoftLink to="mysoft-industry-manufacturing" position="body">Sage X3 for manufacturers at Mysoft</MysoftLink> for examples.

## Cost and implementation

NetSuite's annual licence combines the core platform, optional modules and users, with a one-time implementation fee. Sage X3 is quoted, and Mysoft publishes a <MysoftLink to="mysoft-x3-cost" position="body">Sage X3 cost calculator</MysoftLink>.
